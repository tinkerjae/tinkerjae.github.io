Overcommitting

(February 16, 2026)
Tags: vent;

I have this weird habit of overcommitting to things.

Well actually no. I’m not going to change the title, but it’s like I get so attatched to certain ideas and since I’m kind of a scatterbrain, I get a lot of ideas that sound good. I try to do all of them and stretch myself thin in the process. As you can likely see, this blog post is 2 days late. This is another example of this phenomenon. I don’t really know what to do about it but I wanted to put out something for the week so, vent post?

You ever hear the phrase “Under promise, over deliver”? That’s probably written as an antithesis to me. I don’t like letting people down. I don’t like letting myself down. But to be frank, I don’t know what to do about the whole situation. There’s a loneliness to that. To trying, and failing, and trying and failing, and seeing the needle move ever so slightly, but falling back into place soon after. I’m tired.
