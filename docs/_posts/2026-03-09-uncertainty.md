I'm not sure about what to write about.

Also, sorry about posting late, I know pretty much no one reads this but I try to keep things on schedule. But let me rephrase that first statement. It's not that I don't know "what" to write about, it's more in what order, and what should I leave out? 

Eh idk, it's late. Here's my stream of conciousness as per usual:

The last couple weeks have been hectic, (see the fact that I missed a post last week) and things are calming down, sort of??? I had a couple thoughts I wanted to share. My daily notes in [obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) are super bloated and essentially contain my entire schedule for the day, which is redundant as I have that in my calendar I regularly use and refer to. I set a rule for myself a day or two before realizing this that I'm only allowed to edit my daily template once every two weeks so in service of this, I have edited my calendar and have been rewriting each individual daily note instead of the template because I couldn't take it anymore. 

Now my day is divided by 3 categories: Obligations, Hangouts, & Endeavors. Obligations are things that don't take much mental effort and generally require me just showing up on time. Examples of this are work, class, personal care like sleep, eating, showers, and other regularly scheduled events. Hangouts are, well fun hangouts. Social events and other things that aren't neccesarily "obligatory" but I still really want to do. These are different mostly to distinguish what I can reschedule with little effort, at least compared to my obligations. Lastly we have, endeavors. Endeavors are things I choose to do that I'm committing to. It's like an obligation but generally takes more effort than just simply showing up. Endeavors are also the primary place I choose to put tasks in now, BOOM! My daily note used to be filled with "tasks" that were really just placeholders for 80% obligations and hangouts. Since those are effectively removed, I only really have to worry about the things I choose to pursue for my own reasons. In this category is stuff like cleaning my room, going to the gym, studying x amount of material, playing guitar for t amount of time, etc. As you can likely see, I want to spend more of my time thinking about stuff like that, I want to prioritize my focus mainly around that. Before I'd see this giant list of "tasks" and be overwhelmed. Now I see my list of tasks and it's basically all stuff I actually have to think about, which definitely removes a lot of that decision or ruminatively based fatigue.

Since only seeing endeavor related tasks, I now also realize how little time I have for myself throughout my days. I won't bore y'all to death with sappy "I have a full time job and do college, woe is me" kind of stuff. I could, but I'm not always interested in stuff like that, especially when it's not currently impeding my ability to tackle the problems in my life. But since I now realize how little time I have for myself, things feel different. I no longer have an entire day to do homework after work. I really only have 2-6 and sometimes MAYBE 8 hours. This gives me a better understanding of my situation, and recontextualizes my entire approach towards these sorts of things. It's early days now, but I really think this will help me value the time I have in my life. I hope it helps me tackle these things to a suitable level.

Oh yeah, in consequence to this change of perspective, I've already realized something. I don't have a consistent daily routine for my hobbies, and barely have one for studying. To fix this, I'm going to try and study for a little (5-30 minutes) bit each morning. I know it's not going to move mountains, but this will help me keep my momentum ([holy shit that's a witty reference](https://tinkerjae.xyz/2026/02/22/momentum.html)) and start my day off strong. On this same theme of small daily habits, I literally have nothing like that for my hobbies so I'm going to implement a small (also 5-30 minutes) daily hobby session at the end of the day before bed. I'm going to do that quite soon so I'll have to wrap up shortly.
